# Privacy Policy — Beast Arena

**Last Updated:** February 2026

## 1. Introduction

Beast Arena ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data You Provide
| Data | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Display name | Account creation | In-game identity |
| Solana wallet address | Wallet connection (optional) | Authentication, transactions |

### 2.2 Data We Collect Automatically
| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Device type & OS version | Compatibility, crash analysis |
| Game statistics (wins, losses, rank) | Leaderboards, matchmaking |
| Match replays | Dispute resolution, anti-cheat |
| IP address | Rate limiting, abuse prevention |
| Anonymous analytics (screen views, session duration) | Product improvement |

### 2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect
- Real name, email, phone number (unless you provide them voluntarily)
- Location data
- Contact lists
- Private keys or wallet seed phrases (we never have access to these)
- Biometric data

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- **Game functionality:** Matchmaking, leaderboards, player profiles
- **Anti-cheat:** Detecting and preventing unfair play
- **Service improvement:** Analytics to improve game balance and performance
- **Communication:** In-game notifications about updates, maintenance, or policy changes
- **Legal compliance:** Responding to legal requests where required

## 4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We may share data with:

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Cloud providers (Fly.io, Neon) | Hosting | Encrypted game data |
| Analytics tools | Product improvement | Anonymous usage stats |
| Law enforcement | Legal obligation | As required by law |

### 4.1 Blockchain Data
- Solana wallet addresses and transaction history are publicly visible on the blockchain by design.
- Match wager transactions are recorded on-chain and are immutable.
- We cannot delete on-chain data.

## 5. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Guest account data | Until account deletion or 12 months of inactivity |
| Match history | 12 months |
| Match replays | 90 days |
| Analytics data | 24 months (anonymized) |
| Server logs | 30 days |

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access** — Request a copy of your data
- **Correction** — Fix inaccurate data
- **Deletion** — Request deletion of your account and data
- **Portability** — Export your data in a standard format
- **Objection** — Object to certain data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@beastarena.gg.

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures:
- Encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS)
- Encrypted database connections
- Hashed and salted authentication tokens
- Access controls on all internal systems
- Regular security reviews (see SECURITY.md)

## 8. Children's Privacy

Beast Arena is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

## 9. International Users

Our servers may be located in regions different from yours. By using the Game, you consent to data transfer to and processing in these regions.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes via in-game notification. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
